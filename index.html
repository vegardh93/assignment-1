Product Overview

The RØDE NT1 5th Generation combines the classic sound of the NT1 with modern recording technology. Featuring a 1-inch condenser capsule and a cardioid pickup pattern, it is designed to capture clear, detailed audio from vocals, instruments, podcasts, and voiceovers.

Its Dual Connect technology allows you to record through a traditional XLR connection or connect directly to a computer using USB-C. Digital recording supports 32-bit float audio and sample rates up to 192 kHz, providing additional flexibility during recording and editing.

With exceptionally low self-noise of just 4 dBA and the ability to handle sound pressure levels up to 142 dB SPL, the microphone performs well with both quiet and loud sound sources.

Key Features

  • Dual Connect Technology: Record through XLR using an audio interface or connect directly to a computer via USB-C.
  • 32-Bit Float Recording: Capture audio with greater flexibility when recording digitally through USB.
  • 1-Inch HF6 Condenser Capsule: Designed to capture detailed and natural sound.
  • Ultra-Low Self-Noise: Just 4 dBA of self-noise for clean recordings.
  • High SPL Handling: Handles sound pressure levels up to 142 dB SPL.
  • Built-In Digital Signal Processing: APHEX audio processing is available through USB at 48 kHz sample rate.
  • Versatile Recording Applications: Suitable for vocals, instruments, podcasts, voiceovers, and studio recordings.
